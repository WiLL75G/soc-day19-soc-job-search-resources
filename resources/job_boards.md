# Cybersecurity Job Boards and Resources

## Job Boards

### Tier 1, Best for Entry Level

| Platform | URL | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | linkedin.com/jobs | Largest network, recruiters search here directly |
| Indeed | indeed.com | Highest volume of Tier 1 postings |
| CyberSecJobs | cybersecjobs.com | Dedicated cybersecurity board |
| Dice | dice.com | Strong for tech and security roles |

### Tier 2, Specialist Platforms

| Platform | URL | Why |
|---|---|---|
| InfoSec Jobs | infosec-jobs.com | Dedicated InfoSec board, good remote filter |
| SANS Jobs | jobs.sans.org | Quality over volume, SANS community roles |
| ISC2 Job Board | isc2.org/career-resources | Roles posted by ISC2 certified hiring managers |
| Cybersecurity Ventures | cybersecurityventures.com/jobs | Industry specific listings |

### Tier 3, Networking and the Hidden Market

| Platform | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| X | #CyberSecJobs, #Hiring | Hiring managers post before HR does |
| Discord | TryHackMe, Blue Team Labs | Community job channels, referral route |
| Reddit | r/cybersecurity, r/netsec | Job posts and referrals |
| GitHub | github.com | The portfolio works while you sleep, inbound not outbound |

Tier 3 is the one people skip and it is the only tier where you are not competing against 400 applicants.

## Applying

```
1. Apply early. New postings get read. Week two
   postings get filtered.

2. Tailor every application. Mirror the exact
   language from the JD. Never send a generic CV.

3. Follow up after 5 business days. A LinkedIn
   message to the hiring manager is the differentiator.

4. Volume matters at entry level, but tailored volume.
   5 tailored beats 20 generic.

5. Track everything. Company, role, date, status,
   contact.
```

Rule 2 and rule 4 pull against each other, and rule 2 wins. The bottleneck is not how many applications go out, it is how many get past the screen.

## Companies Hiring Entry Level

### MSSPs, the entry point

- Secureworks
- Arctic Wolf
- Trustwave
- Optiv
- Herjavec Group

MSSPs hire Tier 1 at volume because their business model requires it. They will take lab work and a portfolio where an in house SOC wants two years experience. This is the door.

### Cleared and government contractors

- Leidos
- Booz Allen Hamilton
- SAIC
- ManTech

Almost all of these require US citizenship and clearance. Listed for completeness, not as targets.

### Tech companies with internal SOCs

- Microsoft
- Amazon, AWS Security
- Google, Chronicle
- Palo Alto Networks
- CrowdStrike
- Splunk

Rarely hire Tier 1 externally. They hire experienced analysts or promote from within. Worth watching, not worth building a strategy around.

### Financial sector

- JP Morgan Chase
- Bank of America
- Citigroup
- Goldman Sachs
- HSBC

High demand, high standards, usually onsite in a financial hub.
